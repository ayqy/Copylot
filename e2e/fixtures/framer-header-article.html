Designing, refining, and maintaining agent skills at Perplexity

Agent skills are reusable packages of instructions, examples, and resources that help agents perform specialized work consistently across a wide range of real user tasks.

Design around real tasks

A useful skill begins with representative work and clear runtime constraints. Authors identify decisions that require judgment and keep deterministic operations in tools.

Refinement comes from reviewing failed runs, separating missing context from weak procedure, and validating each focused revision against related cases.

Maintain quality over time

Teams review skill changes as product changes so users receive more reliable outcomes without needing to understand the implementation details behind the system.

Long-form guidance remains useful when ownership is clear, dependencies are checked, and the examples continue to reflect current workflows rather than historical assumptions.
